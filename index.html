About these visualizations

Remember watching Windows Media Player's Now Playing screen? This is a free, fan-made tribute that recreates its visualization collections in your browser: Bars and Waves (Bars, Scope, Ocean Mist, Fire Storm), Ambience, Battery, Alchemy, Plenoptic and Musical Colors. It also adds four modern ones (Neon Terrain, Radial Pulse, Particle Flow and Beat Radar, which shows the beat tracker predicting the next beats before they sound), hundreds of Milkdrop presets, the retro Classic Spectrum and Oscilloscope, Light Ribbons, and music-reactive takes on classic screensavers: the bouncing DVD logo (yes, it celebrates when it hits the corner), Starfield, Mystify, 3D Pipes, 3D Maze, 3D Text, Bezier, Flying Toasters, Digital Rain, Bliss and the Blue Screen. Plus internet-classic memes that dance exactly on the beat: Rainbow Cat, Party Parrot, Dancing Baby, Vibing Cat and Doge.

Use your microphone, share a browser tab with its sound, or open a music file. No mic? Pick a music style (Techno, House, Hip-hop, Trap, Drum & bass, Party, Rock, Chill) and the visuals move to that kind of music silently, perfect for a TV or a party. On a smart TV it switches to a full-screen remote-control mode, and there are apps for Android TV, LG and Samsung. To visualize all of your computer's sound on a Mac, select a loopback device such as BlackHole as the microphone. Audio never leaves your device. Keys: Space play or pause, F full screen, Left and Right change visualization, R random mode, N next Milkdrop preset, T switch the 2000s look on or off. The address bar always links to the visualization you are watching, so you can share it.
